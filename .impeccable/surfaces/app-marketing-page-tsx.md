# Homepage surface brief

Scope: the homepage (`app/(marketing)/page.tsx`), and since 2026-11-07 the whole marketing site in the same system: the eight inner pages (About, The Course, Amenities, Events, Membership, Stay & Play, Book, Contact), the header (a flagstick rail on wide screens, a top bar, a full-screen menu sheet), the footer, the first-visit loading putt and the error pages (404, route error, global error). Visitor mode: Persuade. Source of truth: the owner's design blueprint "Beyond the 18th" (`C:\Users\ddant\Downloads\Beyond_the_18th_Design_Blueprint.docx`), to be followed to the letter, in a Swiss-style, high-end layout. It replaces the Continuous Putt Journey (2026-11-06).

Audience and action: prospective members (Kenyan executives, founders, high-net-worth individuals). Hero primary action: Explore the club (owner's choice of the blueprint's two options); secondary: Discover the course. Membership (apply) and booking (tee time, M-Pesa) carry chapters 05 and 07.

Owner decisions (2026-11-07): no drawn line in the hero, the ball flies it instead; the drive ends on hole 1 of the 3D course, where its camera starts; the course fills the screen in chapter 03; the loading putt plays once a visit, only while the page loads (capped near two seconds), skippable and never under reduced motion; the inner pages move into the blueprint system, each with its own fairway-line storyline from the blueprint's section 4.

Owner decisions (2026-11-06): blueprint palette on the homepage (Forest #102F25, Club green #214A39, Antique gold #B89556, Warm ivory #E8DFCD, Charcoal #252A25, Sage #52705C, Off-white #F5F1E9, Stone #777E76); "The Reserve" stays the club's name; "Beyond the 18th" is the concept's title.

Proof on hand: live course figures from the database, the 3D course explorer with hole selection, the working booking and community tools. Members, open games, availability and itineraries shown on the homepage are sample data and are labelled.

Constraints from the blueprint: one immersive moment (the course flyover, chapter 03), loaded only when reached, with direct hole selection, reset and a 2D fallback; horizontal scrolling for one chapter only (04), with visible progress, controls and a mobile swipe alternative; everything readable without animation or WebGL; WCAG 2.2 AA (muted text uses charcoal, not Stone, where Stone fails contrast).

Unresolved: the chapter copy beyond the blueprint's hero lines is a draft until the owner approves it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The course is a story you enter: eight numbered chapters of one day at the club, set on a strict Swiss grid, where a single fine antique-gold fairway line carries the eye from the first invitation to the footer. It refuses the luxury template of a photo hero over a row of equal cards, and gold everywhere.

OWN-WORLD: Forest #102F25 and warm ivory #E8DFCD bands alternating dark and editorial, club green #214A39 for interaction, antique gold #B89556 only for the fairway line and premium accents, charcoal #252A25 text on light. Cormorant Garamond display set flush left at the blueprint scale (104/76/60/44/28 desktop), Manrope for everything functional, tabular numerals for figures and times. Hairline rules, a 12/6/4-column grid with 24/20/16px gutters, square-edged buttons, authentic landscape photography, no cards for their own sake.

STORY: The visitor arrives before the first tee, reads the land, flies the eighteen, sees the club in motion through one day, meets the people and how membership works, considers staying longer, makes a move (book, pass, event, M-Pesa), and leaves with the day still open: contact, WhatsApp, footer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed aerial still under a deep-green overlay. On the grid: the chapter index "01 — The invitation" in the left two columns; the H1 "Read the green. Then make your move." in Cormorant at Display XL across columns 3 to 11, flush left; the blueprint's supporting line in columns 3 to 7; "Explore the club" (antique gold, square) and "Discover the course" (gold-underlined link). A photoreal 3D golf ball sits on its tee above the copy, lit by the photograph's afternoon sun and casting its shadow down and to the right like the trees around it; there is no drawn line in the hero. Scroll, and the ball is driven along the old fairway line's path and out of frame (owner request, 2026-11-06). A hairline info bar at the foot: club name, Kenya, and live holes, par and yards in tabular figures.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction (the owner's blueprint plus "Swiss style"), no roll this round. Signature motif: the fairway line, used as the blueprint's transition grammar: in the hero the ball flies it (the line itself is no longer drawn there), then drops out of the land photograph's sky, lands on its horizon line with two short hops and rolls along it off the picture's end, its shadow and colour matched to that photograph's golden light; it continues as each chapter's rule (its gold length is the story's progress, 1/8 per chapter), follows the horizon in 02; in 03 the course fills the screen, edge to edge, photoreal but cheap (drawn only when it moves), and holds while you scroll: the same ball drops onto hole 1's green and rolls up by the pin, then the camera rises from that green to the whole course, after which the line becomes the selected hole's 3D route; threads the day's moments in 04, branches in 05, becomes a journey route in 06 and a stepper in 07, and resolves into the club mark in 08.
