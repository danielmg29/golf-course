`public/textures/green-contours.svg` is generated by `greenTileSvg()` in `features/journey/green.ts`. To regenerate it:

```bash
npx tsx -e "import('./features/journey/green.ts').then(({greenTileSvg})=>require('node:fs').writeFileSync('public/textures/green-contours.svg', greenTileSvg()))"
```

The photographs on the site are Unsplash stock, listed in `lib/photos.ts`, and are placeholders.
